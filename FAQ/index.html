Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about bidding, buying, and consigning with Fabyle.

Bidding & Buying

How do I register to bid in an auction?
To register, click the "Register to Bid" button on any upcoming auction page. You will need to create a free Fabyle account and put a valid credit card on file. This card is only used for identity verification; you will not be charged unless you win a lot and choose to pay via that card.
What is a Buyer's Premium?
A Buyer's Premium is an additional fee added to the final "hammer price" (the winning bid amount) of an auction lot. Fabyle uses a transparent, tiered structure: 15% for items under $1,000, 10% for items $1,001 - $10,000, and 5% for items $10,001 and above.

Consigning & Selling

How do I submit an item for consignment?
Simply navigate to our "Sell" page and fill out the submission form with a description and photos of your item. One of our acquisitions experts will review the submission and contact you within 48 hours to discuss estimates, grading needs, and shipping logistics.
When and how do I get paid for my sold items?
Consignor settlements are processed 30 to 45 days after the close of the auction, provided the buyer has submitted full, cleared payment. We offer payouts via check, ACH direct deposit, or domestic wire transfer.

Shipping & Vaulting

How much does shipping cost?
Shipping costs are calculated at the end of the auction and appended to your final invoice. The cost depends on the size, weight, destination, and the mandatory transit insurance required to cover the final hammer price of the item. We ship securely using FedEx and USPS Registered Mail.

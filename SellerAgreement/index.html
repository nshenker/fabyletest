Seller Consignment Agreement

2912 Avenue T, Brooklyn, NY 11229 | 917.881.4161 | consign@fabyle.com

Disclaimer: Fabyle LLC recommends that both parties consult with legal counsel to ensure full compliance with the New York City Department of Consumer and Worker Protection (DCWP) and the Uniform Commercial Code (UCC).

This Consignment Agreement is entered into by and between Fabyle LLC ("Fabyle") and the Consignor listed on Page 1.

1. Authority & Exclusive Agency

Consignor grants Fabyle exclusive right to authenticate, grade, market, and sell the Property (Page 1) at public auction/private sale for 90 days from receipt.

2. Representations & Warranties (Clear Title)

Consignor represents and warrants they are the sole, rightful owner of the Property, possess unencumbered title, and the items are free from any liens, claims, or encumbrances. Consignor expressly warrants that the items are not stolen, forged, counterfeit, or subject to any pending law enforcement investigation.

3. Grading & Authentication

Fabyle reserves the right to submit "raw" items to 3rd-party services (PSA, BGS, CGC, etc.). Grading fees are advanced by Fabyle and subsequently deducted from the Consignor's settlement proceeds. Fabyle retains sole discretion over which items warrant grading.

4. Commission & Fees

Fabyle shall deduct the commission selected on Page 1 from the Hammer Price of each sold lot. The Consignor acknowledges that Fabyle also charges the Buyer a "Buyer's Premium," which is retained entirely by Fabyle.

5. Insurance & Risk of Loss

Fabyle assumes the risk of loss from receipt until delivery to the Buyer or return to the Consignor, covered by Fabyle's fine arts/collectibles insurance at fair market value. Fabyle is not liable for minor, pre-existing damage, natural degradation, or damage to third-party graded "slabs" that do not affect the internal collectible.

6. Reserves & Estimates

Unless expressly agreed otherwise, all items are sold without a reserve (absolute auction). If a reserve is listed on Page 1, Fabyle will not sell the lot for less than the reserve amount. Pre-auction estimates are statements of opinion, not guarantees of final sale price.

7. Settlement & Payment

Settlement statements and payments are provided 30-45 days after the auction's close, contingent upon Fabyle receiving full, cleared payment from the Buyer. If a Buyer defaults, Fabyle reserves the right to cancel the sale and re-offer the Property.

8. Unsold Lots & Withdrawal

Once Property is processed, the Consignor may not withdraw it prior to auction. Unauthorized withdrawal results in a penalty equal to 20% of the lot's median pre-auction estimate plus incurred fees. Unsold lots may be re-offered or returned at the Consignor's expense.

9. Law Enforcement Cooperation & Title Disputes

Fabyle actively cooperates with all law enforcement agencies. If Fabyle is notified, served with a subpoena, or reasonably suspects that an item may be stolen, illicitly acquired, or subject to a title dispute, Fabyle reserves the absolute right to: (a) withhold the item from auction; (b) freeze settlement funds; and/or (c) surrender the item to authorities. Fabyle shall bear no liability to the Consignor for taking such actions.

10. Dispute Resolution & Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York. Any disputes, claims, or controversies arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be resolved through binding arbitration in Kings County (Brooklyn), New York, administered in accordance with the commercial rules of the American Arbitration Association. The parties expressly waive their right to a trial by jury. The prevailing party shall be entitled to recover its reasonable attorney's fees and costs.

What We Accept at Auction

Fabyle specializes in curating the finest pop culture and sports artifacts. We meticulously authenticate and evaluate every item to ensure our bidders have access to only the highest quality inventory. We are currently accepting consignments for:

  • Sports Cards & Memorabilia

    Graded vintage and modern rookie cards, game-worn jerseys, signed equipment, and historic tickets.

  • Trading Card Games (TCG)

    High-grade Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering (MTG), Yu-Gi-Oh!, and sealed vintage booster boxes.

  • Comic Books & Original Art

    CGC/CBCS graded Golden, Silver, and Bronze Age comics, as well as original published illustration art.

  • Movie Props & Pop Culture

    Screen-used film props, rare vinyl records, historical documents, and significant pop-culture artifacts.

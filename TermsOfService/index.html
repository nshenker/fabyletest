  • Bidding as a Binding Contract: Placing a bid constitutes a legally binding offer to purchase the lot at the bid price, plus the Buyer’s Premium, applicable taxes, and shipping/insurance costs. Bids cannot be retracted once submitted.

  • Buyer’s Premium: A Buyer’s Premium will be added to the hammer price (winning bid) of each lot. Our Buyer's Premium is tiered based on the final hammer price: 15% for items under $1,000; 10% for items between $1,001 and $10,000; and 5% for items $10,001 and above.

  • Invoicing: Invoices are transmitted electronically within 48 hours of the auction’s close.

  • Accepted Payment Methods: We accept wire transfers, ACH, and major credit cards. Credit card payments are subject to a 3% processing fee and are capped at a maximum of $5000 per invoice. Invoices exceeding this amount must be paid via wire transfer or ACH.
  • Non-Payment: If a Buyer fails to pay, Fabyle reserves the right to cancel the sale, resell the collectible, charge a late fee of 2% per week, and permanently ban the Buyer from future Fabyle auctions.

Fabyle makes no guarantee that a raw item will achieve a specific grade if subsequently submitted to a third-party grading company. Returns will not be accepted because a raw item received a lower-than-expected grade.
